# Ownership — geospatial data, GIS and validation (Karthik)

I own:
- Geographic data acquisition: DEMs, rivers/waterways, roads/crossings, settlements/villages, buildings (where useful), shelters (only where reliable info exists).
- GIS processing: DEM coverage/quality inspection, nodata and water-body artefacts, terrain rasters, HAND preprocessing support, geographic artifacts, CRS documentation, format coordination with backend.
- Validation: dataset provenance, resolution/coverage, coordinate sanity, geographic consistency, reproducible synthetic disaster scenarios, QA of flood and road outputs.
- Presentation: technical diagrams, data-source documentation, validation results, known limitations, demonstration narrative.

I do NOT:
- Modify backend or frontend implementation.
- Represent synthetic observations as real observations.
- Invent shelter locations or capacities.
- Claim DEM-derived flood projections are validated historical reconstructions.
